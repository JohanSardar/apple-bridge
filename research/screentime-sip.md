# Screen Time data and SIP on macOS 26

Research date: **2026-10-16**. **All findings are unverified on the user's Mac/iPhone:** this was web/source research, without device testing. “Documented” means Apple documentation; “reported” means another researcher's observation; “inference” identifies an extrapolation.

**Verdict:** SIP-off **probably permits access to the vaulted files** (moderate confidence), but **is not a verified recipe for obtaining every app's exact Mac and iPhone duration on Tahoe** (low confidence in completeness/equality). File access and reconstructing Apple's totals are separate questions. Current DeviceActivity summaries are the strongest extraction target; simply unlocking RMAdminStore may yield settings and empty usage tables. Apple's engineer confirms SIP-off access to Data Vaults generally, although that example predates Tahoe. [Apple DTS](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/114452), [backup project](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-backup).

**Locations and contents — reported, version-dependent**

Resolve the user's directory with `getconf DARWIN_USER_DIR`; define `S=<that-directory>/com.apple.ScreenTimeAgent/Store`. Its expanded location is `/private/var/folders/<xx>/<hash>/0/com.apple.ScreenTimeAgent/Store`.

- **`S/RMAdminStore-Local.sqlite` and `S/RMAdminStore-Cloud.sqlite`**, plus SQLite WAL/SHM companions: historical Core Data aggregates include app/domain seconds and device/user identity, including synchronized devices. However, the backup project's current README describes these as a device/settings registry with unused usage tables. “Cloud” does not establish that iPhone usage is in that database. [mac_apt source](https://github.com/ydkhatri/mac_apt/blob/master/plugins/screentime.py), [README](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-backup).
- **`S/Library/com.apple.DeviceActivity/`**: binary summary plists, including `Cloud/<user>/<device>/{Daily,Hourly}/ActivitySegments/*.plist`; the backup author also reports Weekly summaries. These contain stored per-app durations, websites, pickups and notifications, with cross-device iPhone data. The parser reads `value.categoryActivities[].applicationActivities[].bundleIdentifier` and `totalActivityDuration` (seconds); website durations are separate. Device identity comes from the directory. These are recorded totals rather than focus-event estimates; the author reports roughly four weeks' retention. Local/Cloud branches must be inspected before deciding which represent the Mac and avoiding duplication. [capture script](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-backup/blob/main/screentime-backup.sh), [parser](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-dashboard/blob/main/src/lib/data/deviceactivity.ts), [observations](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-dashboard/blob/main/AGENTS.md).
- **`~/Library/Application Support/Knowledge/knowledgeC.db`**: `ZOBJECT` rows with `ZSTREAMNAME='/app/usage'`; bundle ID in `ZVALUESTRING`, interval in `ZSTARTDATE/ZENDDATE`. It can contain synchronized events historically, but Share Across Devices does **not** guarantee current iPhone records here. The dashboard author found useful Mac intervals in laptop snapshots through July 2026, but an empty newer Mac database. Readability does not establish continued population. [APOLLO](https://github.com/mac4n6/APOLLO/blob/master/modules/knowledge_app_usage.txt), [observations](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-dashboard/blob/main/AGENTS.md).
- **`~/Library/Biome/streams/restricted/App.InFocus/{local,remote/<device-UUID>}/`**: SEGB focus transitions; remote streams can contain iPhone events. Related streams include `App.Activity`, `App.WebUsage`, `App.MediaUsage`, and **`ScreenTime.AppUsage`** where present. The latter's name does not prove stored duration totals: forensic research describes bundle/event records, and an available parser explicitly labels its schemas inferred. [backup script](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-backup/blob/main/screentime-backup.sh), [forensic stream research](https://blog.digital-forensics.it/2026/07/84-streams-later-part-2-inside-apple.html), [parser limitations](https://github.com/ForensicFoundry/biome-parser).

**Protection and encryption**

Reported: Full Disk Access permits knowledgeC/Biome collection. The backup author measured ScreenTimeAgent-store denial even with FDA on **26.3**, while **26.1** allowed access. Its “≥26.3” statement is an author generalization, not an Apple-documented transition or proof for every 26.x build. DeviceActivity inherits the inaccessible parent. [version report](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-backup).

Documented: Data Vaults require private entitlements; root and FDA alone are insufficient. Some have `UF_DATAVAULT`, but the flag's absence does not disprove vault protection. **Unverified:** actual `com.apple.rootless` attributes, `restricted` flags and exact enforcement hooks on this user's stores; EPERM alone cannot identify the layer. Sandbox permissions remain distinct; the Tahoe researcher identifies the private `com.apple.private.screen-time.persistence` entitlement. [research](https://dunkirk.sh/blog/screentime/). [Apple DTS](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/114452).

Inference: an unsandboxed FDA-granted reader, after SIP-off, should bypass ordinary vault enforcement; root may additionally satisfy ownership permissions. **No evidence found that disabling AMFI is required merely to read these files.** This does not establish that an entitlement-forged app or private XPC call will work.

Documented: Screen Time synchronization uses end-to-end encryption. That is distinct from local-file encryption. Plain SQLite queries and binary-plist parsers demonstrate readable acquired artifacts, but no source established Tahoe-specific SQLCipher, keychain-wrapped database keys or file-protection classes. Those remain **unverified**. SIP-off does not itself unlock FileVault, obtain an absent iCloud key or recover unsynchronized records. [Apple Screen Time security](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/security/secd8831e732/web).

**Evidence of SIP-off extraction**

Kieran Klukas's September 2026 write-up combines macOS 15.3 static analysis with live Tahoe **26.3.1** investigation. It reports root/FDA denial and asserts SIP-off makes DB dumping straightforward, but supplies **no SIP-off per-app/per-device duration export or comparison with Settings**. Its demonstrated plaintext query instead reads a PIN through a Time Machine snapshot. [original research](https://dunkirk.sh/blog/screentime/).

A June 2026 [forum user](https://www.reddit.com/r/MacOS/comments/1tteg4s/screen_time_only_shows_all_usage_no_per_app/) reports clearing Screen Time files with SIP disabled, without exported totals or a precise OS build. Targeted searches found no stronger macOS 15/26 reproduction in GitHub issues, APOLLO, mac_apt, Sarah Edwards/mac4n6, Hexordia or Cellebrite. These establish artifact interpretation, not contemporary acquisition with SIP disabled. [APOLLO schema](https://github.com/mac4n6/APOLLO/blob/master/modules/screentime_timed_items.txt), [Hexordia](https://www.hexordia.com/blog/brewing-with-ipas), [Cellebrite's older iOS extraction notes](https://cellebrite.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/ReleaseNotes_UFED_PA7.29_A4.pdf). Absence from this search is not proof no example exists.

**Schema and daily calculation**

For populated historical RMAdminStore schemas, join:

`ZUSAGETIMEDITEM.ZCATEGORY → ZUSAGECATEGORY.Z_PK`;
`ZUSAGECATEGORY.ZBLOCK → ZUSAGEBLOCK.Z_PK`;
`ZUSAGEBLOCK.ZUSAGE → ZUSAGE.Z_PK`;
`ZUSAGE.ZDEVICE → ZCOREDEVICE.Z_PK`, and `ZUSAGE.ZUSER → ZCOREUSER.Z_PK`.

Filter the intended user, app bundle IDs and one nonoverlapping block granularity; sum **`ZUSAGETIMEDITEM.ZTOTALTIMEINSECONDS`** by device, app and local day. Core Data dates require **+978307200** to obtain Unix seconds. Inspect `ZDURATIONINMINUTES` before selecting hourly blocks; do not sum hourly and daily totals together, or blindly union Local/Cloud duplicates. Match the recorded timezone, including DST. This recipe is **unverified on 26.x**, whose usage tables may be empty. [APOLLO query](https://github.com/mac4n6/APOLLO/blob/master/modules/screentime_timed_items.txt), [Plaso schema](https://plaso.readthedocs.io/en/latest/sources/api/plaso.parsers.sqlite_plugins.html).

For DeviceActivity, use daily per-app `totalActivityDuration`, retain user/device/window identity, and select the latest replacement for each segment. Do not add Daily, Hourly and Weekly representations together. Preserve original precision rather than the dashboard parser's rounded output. [parser](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-dashboard/blob/main/src/lib/data/deviceactivity.ts).

**Alternatives with SIP enabled**

- **Best route to Apple's displayed totals for both devices:** System Settings → Screen Time → App & Website Activity; select each device, date and Show Apps. Accessibility automation is a plausible extraction route with permission, **unverified on Tahoe**. Capture every row; UI rounding may prevent exact seconds. [Apple's Tahoe instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/mac-help/mchle37ec855/26/mac/26).
- **Raw totals:** accessible older snapshots/26.1 DeviceActivity captures are promising. Recovery/offline reading of an unlocked Data volume is an **untested candidate**, not a guaranteed workflow. The published Time Machine bypass is version-sensitive: Apple records its snapshot-permissions fix as CVE-2026-65380; do not assume current backups bypass vaults. [research](https://dunkirk.sh/blog/screentime/), [Apple fix](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149035).
- **knowledgeC/Biome:** useful fallback, not proven identical to Screen Time. Sum clipped `ZENDDATE-ZSTARTDATE` intervals only where populated. Biome pairing loses certainty with missing transitions; the dashboard infers endings and caps sessions at four hours. No published error bound against Apple totals was found. [dashboard observations](https://github.com/alexjmiller5/screentime-dashboard/blob/main/AGENTS.md).
- **APIs:** signing an app does not confer Apple's private ScreenTimeCore/UsageTracking entitlements; no verified private-framework exporter was found. Ordinary DeviceActivity reports sandbox sensitive data. A significant newer option is **iOS/iPadOS 26.4+ `DeviceActivityData.activityData(filteredBy:using:)`**, expressly intended for export, with `com.apple.developer.family-controls.app-and-website-usage` and `approvedWithDataAccess` authorization; development/testing works in any region, customer use requires EU location/account. This is a promising iPhone route, **not a documented native Mac export**. [private-service research](https://dunkirk.sh/blog/screentime/), [report restrictions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivityreport), [export API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/activitydata(filteredby:using:)).

**SIP-off costs and partial disabling**

Documented: Apple Silicon SIP-off entails Permissive Security and weakens kernel protection. Reported consequences include App Store iPhone/iPad apps refusing launch, Apple Pay becoming unavailable, and premium DRM video losing 4K. [Apple boot policy](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/security/sec7d92dc49f/web), [utility developer's observed effects](https://github.com/cormiertyshawn895/RecordingIndicatorUtility), [Apple Pay guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118268).

**Unverified:** whether `csrutil enable --without fs` alone opens Tahoe's ScreenTime vault. It is the relevant candidate; NVRAM-only disabling does not remove filesystem policy. Apple defines filesystem, NVRAM and authenticated-root controls separately. No evidence requires disabling authenticated root or FileVault for this read-only task. Partial disabling can still affect app compatibility. [Apple CSR definitions](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/main/bsd/sys/csr.h), [firsthand partial-SIP report](https://gist.github.com/b0gdanw/b349f5f72097955cf18d6e7d8035c665?permalink_comment_id=5613296).
